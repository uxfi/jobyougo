4. Personal data

The controller is [Legal name of the seller], reachable at chilka.v@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

  • When you order or write to us: your name and email, the link and the brief you send, whether you buy as a business or as an individual, your country, and for a business its name and VAT number. We use them to answer you, confirm the scope, carry out the order and invoice it. Legal basis: the steps before a contract and the contract itself, and for invoices our legal obligations.
  • When you order: the order form sends your order (package, options, brief, link, name, email, how you order, country, VAT number) to our server, which emails it to us and sends you a confirmation through our email provider. It is kept in those two emails, in mailboxes hosted by Google (Gmail), and as one line in the server log. If sending fails, the form prepares the email in your own email app instead.
  • When you pay by card: your order and your details go to our server and to Stripe, which takes the payment and issues the invoice. You enter your card on Stripe’s page; we never see it.
  • When you use the assistant: your messages go to our server and, through OpenRouter, to an AI model that writes the answer. Our server does not save the conversation. Please do not share sensitive information in it. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering visitors’ questions.
  • When you book a call: Calendly handles the booking, under its own privacy policy.
  • Technical data: like any website, the host receives your IP address and the pages you request, to deliver them and keep the site secure. Our server also uses your IP address briefly, in memory only, to limit repeated orders and messages.

Who receives it

  • The host: [Name of the host].
  • Stripe, for payments and invoices.
  • Google, for email.
  • OpenRouter and the AI model provider it routes the request to, for the assistant.
  • Calendly, for booking calls.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers rely on the safeguards they offer, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Invoices and order records: 10 years, as French accounting law requires.
  • Emails about a project that did not go ahead: [Retention period to set].
  • The order draft in your browser: 14 days at most (section 5).

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to chilka.v@gmail.com: we answer within one month, as the GDPR requires. You can also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority.

5. Storage in your browser

These pages set no advertising or analytics cookies. They keep a few things in your own browser, which never leave it unless you send an order:

  • your language choice (v2studio-lang);
  • the order you are writing (v2studio-order-draft), for 14 days at most. It is deleted once the order is sent or when you leave for the payment page;
  • while you are on Stripe’s payment page, the order itself (v2studio-order), for that browser tab only, so that a cancelled payment brings you back to the same order.

Stripe’s payment page and Calendly set their own cookies, under their own policies.
