7. How the work runs

The steps below are those of a Redesign.

  • Day 0: a 30-minute call or a written brief. Scope, price and dates are confirmed in writing.
  • Day 1: Hugo goes through your page as a first-time customer, on phone and laptop, compares it with your closest competitors, and writes down every place where people get stuck, ranked by impact.
  • Day 2: you see the direction: the structure, one key screen and the tone. You approve it, or ask for changes before the build. This is the moment to say it is not right: we change the direction before building.
  • Days 3 and 4: the full page in code, desktop and mobile, with your real content and every state.
  • Day 5: the 12-point check, then delivery with the source, the completed grid and the handoff notes. Your revision rounds start.

A Build follows the same steps and is delivered on Day 7. About 30 days after delivery, a follow-up call looks at your numbers with you. It needs access to your analytics.

8. Revisions and fixes

  • A Redesign includes 1 round. A Build includes 2 rounds, plus small fixes for 14 days after delivery.
  • Rounds and fixes are to be used within 14 days of delivery. Rounds not used by then lapse.
  • A new page, a new feature or a new direction after Day 2 is a new request: an add-on at its posted price, or a quote.
  • An extra revision round costs $90.
