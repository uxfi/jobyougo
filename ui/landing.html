Principles

Built to say no — and to leave the last word to you.

Signal over volume. JobYouGo is a filter, not a spray-and-pray auto-applier.

It never submits for you

It pre-fills every field and drafts every answer. You review, you click Submit — always.

It tells you to skip weak fits

Below 4.0/5 it recommends not applying. You can override it — and it will say so.

It reformulates, never fabricates

Your CV is rewritten from your real experience. Numbers must trace back to your own stories.

Your data stays yours

Open source, bring your own AI key. Your CV, reports and tracker stay in your workspace.
