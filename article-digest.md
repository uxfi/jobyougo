## Creads.io case study: approved factual material

**Context and role.** Creads.io is an independent AI advertising SaaS founded and built by Hugo since 2024. The work connects a brand's information with a creative brief and generated ad assets. Hugo owns the product design, AI workflow, infrastructure and launch.

**Brand context.** A Brand Agent scrapes the brand website with Firecrawl and structures its identity, tone, audience and positioning as JSON. Persistent memory through Mem0 keeps brand context available across sessions. RAG retrieves taxonomy data and brand history for generation.

**Creative decisions.** A structured brief specifies the hook, emotion, bias, composition, format, awareness stage, campaign goal, wording rules, platform and audience. SaaS and e-commerce use separate briefing logic. Matching draws on 500+ taxonomy elements across 6 dimensions and 4,000+ structured templates.

**Checking outputs.** One prompt generates an output; a second checks coherence and compliance with the brief. This describes the implemented checking mechanism. No benchmark, error rate, measured conversion uplift or reliability guarantee is recorded.

**Delivery and traction.** Python workers run on Google Cloud Run, with Supabase, Vercel and Cloudflare. As confirmed by Hugo on 2026-09-10: 1,200 registered users, 20 paying clients and 5,000 monthly visits. Zero paid acquisition is recorded in the existing CV. Revenue, retention, active-user counts and exact analytics windows are not documented.
