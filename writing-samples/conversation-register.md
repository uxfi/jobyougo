# Conversation register (Cursor chats with Hugo)

Not public copy. Used only to capture how he actually talks when he writes to an agent: spoken French, short, factual, little ceremony.

Collected 2026-09-17 from local career-ops / jobyougo chats.

## How he writes here

- Voice-typed or dashed off: fillers, repeats, English words inside French (cover letter, bootstrap, workflow, matching).
- Corrects facts in one pass, no preamble.
- Directives are short: "oui", "continue", "d autres choses qu on peut ameliorer ?"
- When something is wrong: names the bug, then the expected behavior. No blame essay.
- Style feedback on generated text: too AI, em dashes, sentences too complex, use the copywriting skills.
- Mixes FR and EN without switching the whole message.

## Register to keep in outgoing text

Spoken-professional. Someone explaining the work to a recruiter in a call, then typing it. Not a pitch deck. Not a tweet thread with "ahah" unless the channel is social.

Cover letters and form answers: same cadence as the posts (short beats, I-did-this, concrete tools), cleaned of fillers and jokes. Match the JD language (FR or EN).
